Patient & Services Guide — 2024 Edition

1. About HealthPlus Medical Group
HealthPlus Medical Group is a multi-specialty healthcare organization founded in 2005,
headquartered in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. With over 1,200 medical professionals and 18 branches
across the Kingdom, HealthPlus delivers world-class preventive, diagnostic, and therapeutic care to
more than 500,000 patients annually. Our vision is to be the most trusted healthcare partner in the
region by combining medical excellence with compassionate patient care.

Our Core Values

• Patient First – Every decision is guided by patient safety and well-being.
• Excellence – We maintain the highest standards of clinical quality.
• Innovation – We adopt the latest evidence-based medical practices.
• Integrity – Transparent, honest, and ethical care at all times.
• Community – Committed to improving public health across the region.

2. Medical Services & Specialties

HealthPlus offers a comprehensive range of outpatient and inpatient medical services across 20+
specialties. Our integrated care model ensures seamless coordination between departments to
provide patients with a complete, continuous care experience.

Primary Care & General Medicine

Our General Practice clinics are available 7 days a week from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Services include
routine check-ups, chronic disease management, vaccinations, minor procedures, health screenings,
and referrals to specialist departments. Walk-in appointments are accepted, though scheduled
bookings are prioritized. Average wait time is under 20 minutes.

Cardiology & Heart Care

The HealthPlus Cardiac Center is equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostics including ECG,
echocardiography, stress testing, Holter monitoring, and coronary angiography. Our team of 12
board-certified cardiologists specializes in heart failure, arrhythmias, hypertension, and coronary
artery disease. The center also operates a 24/7 chest pain unit for emergency cardiac evaluation.

Orthopedics & Sports Medicine

From sports injuries and fractures to joint replacements and spine surgery, our Orthopedic
Department provides the full spectrum of musculoskeletal care. We use minimally invasive
arthroscopic techniques to reduce recovery times. The dedicated Sports Medicine clinic serves


athletes of all levels, offering physiotherapy, rehabilitation, and performance optimization programs.

Oncology & Cancer Care

The HealthPlus Cancer Center offers multidisciplinary tumor board consultations, chemotherapy,
targeted therapy, immunotherapy, and radiation oncology services. We are affiliated with international
cancer research networks, giving patients access to clinical trials and cutting-edge treatments. Our
oncology nurses provide personalized support throughout the treatment journey.
Women's Health & Obstetrics
HealthPlus Women's Health Center provides comprehensive gynecological care, prenatal and
antenatal services, high-risk pregnancy management, labor and delivery, and postpartum support.
Our fertility clinic offers IVF, IUI, and reproductive endocrinology services. All consultations are
conducted in a private, comfortable environment with female physicians available upon request.

Pediatrics & Child Health

Our Pediatrics Department caters to children from birth to 18 years. Services include newborn care,
growth monitoring, vaccination schedules per MOH guidelines, developmental assessments, and
management of childhood illnesses. The child-friendly clinic environment is designed to minimize
anxiety and ensure a positive healthcare experience for young patients.

5. Patient Wellness & Chronic Disease Programs

Diabetes Management Program

Our certified Diabetes Care team provides individualized management plans combining
endocrinology consultations, diabetes education, nutritional counseling, foot care clinics, and
continuous glucose monitoring. The program targets HbA1c optimization and reduces hospital
admissions from diabetic complications by 40% among enrolled patients.

Hypertension & Heart Health Program

Patients with high blood pressure are enrolled in a structured follow-up program with quarterly
cardiologist reviews, 24-hour ambulatory blood pressure monitoring, lifestyle modification coaching,
and medication adherence support. The program has demonstrated a 35% improvement in blood
pressure control rates compared to standard care.

Weight Management & Nutrition Clinic

The HealthPlus Weight Management Clinic offers medically supervised programs including dietary
assessment, caloric planning, behavioral therapy, pharmacological support, and bariatric surgery
evaluation. Registered dietitians provide personalized meal plans. The average patient achieves
8–12% body weight reduction within 6 months.

Mental Health & Wellbeing Services


HealthPlus provides confidential mental health services including psychiatry, clinical psychology, and
counseling. We treat depression, anxiety disorders, PTSD, OCD, ADHD, and substance use
disorders. Telepsychiatry appointments are available for patients who prefer remote access. All
sessions are fully confidential and HIPAA-compliant.

7. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What documents do I need to bring?
A: Bring your national ID or Iqama, insurance card (if applicable), and any previous medical
records or test results relevant to your visit.
Q: Does HealthPlus offer home visits?
A: Yes. HealthPlus Home Health services are available in Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam for
post-operative care, elderly patients, and chronic disease management. Book via the app or call
center.
Q: How long does a referral to a specialist take?
A: Internal referrals to HealthPlus specialists are typically processed within 24 hours. Urgent
referrals are handled the same day.
Q: Are second opinions available?
A: Yes. HealthPlus Second Opinion Clinic is available for oncology, cardiac surgery, and
neurosurgery cases. International second opinions can also be arranged through our partnerships
with Mayo Clinic and Cleveland Clinic.
Q: What is the policy for pediatric patients?
A: Children under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian. Patients aged 15–17
may attend mental health consultations without a guardian upon written parental consent.
Q: Is my medical data kept private?


A: All patient data is stored on encrypted servers in compliance with Saudi PDPL regulations.
HealthPlus does not share personal health information with third parties without explicit consent.
Q: Does HealthPlus have a loyalty program?
A: Yes. The HealthPlus+ Loyalty Program rewards points for every visit and lab test. Points can be
redeemed for discounts on future consultations, pharmacy purchases, and health packages.
